Welcome to my page!

I am an astronomer originally from the Republic of Korea. I came to the U.S. in 2009 to pursue bachelor's degree in Astronomy at the Pennsylvania State University and since then, I've been living in the U.S. 
I majored in Astronomy & Astrophysics and Physics and minored in Mathematics in undergrad. I finished my bachelor’s degree in only 7 semesters. I worked as a research assistant in the Astronomy & Astrophysics department at Pennsylvania State University under the supervision of Prof. Jason Wright.
I came to Boston in 2014 for graduate school where I got my Ph.D in Astronomy from Boson University in July of 2020 under the advise of Prof. Philip Muirhead. Now I am the Immersion GRating INfrared Spectrometer (IGRINS) postdoctoral fellow at the University of Texas at Austin. 

My research interest lies on studying and characterizing low-mass stars, stars that are cooler and smaller than our own Sun.
